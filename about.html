About

I’m an undergraduate at MIT studying Physics and Electrical Engineering with Computing. I’m drawn to systems, signals, and electromagnetics—the places where hardware and computation meet.

I like working across the whole system, from circuit design and simulation to low-level programming. I’m always trying to learn something new, and this site is where I keep a growing collection of that work.
